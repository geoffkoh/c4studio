# 1. Record architecture decisions

Date: 2026-01-15

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Decisions in this codebase have been reversed more than once on the
strength of a comment that turned out to be an assertion rather than a
measurement. Where the reasoning is not written down, it gets re-derived —
usually differently.

## Decision

We will record architecturally significant decisions as ADRs in this
directory, and state the evidence, not just the conclusion.

## Consequences

A decision that cannot be justified in a paragraph is probably not
understood well enough to make yet.
